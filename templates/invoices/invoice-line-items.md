# Invoice Line Items

Create each of these as a saved Invoice Item/Product in SuiteDash (Settings → Invoicing → Items) so you're never retyping them.

## 1. Deposit Invoice — 50%

> **Video Production Services — Deposit (50%)**
> Project: [Project Name]
> Deposit due prior to production start, per signed agreement dated [Date].
> Amount: $[50% of total]
> Due: Upon signing

## 2. Final Invoice — Remaining Balance

> **Video Production Services — Final Balance**
> Project: [Project Name]
> Remaining balance due upon final delivery, per signed agreement dated [Date].
> Amount: $[50% of total]
> Terms: Net 7

## 3. Add-On / Change Order Invoice

> **Video Production — Additional Services**
> Project: [Project Name]
> Description: [e.g. Additional revision round / Rush delivery / Extra location / Additional cutdown format]
> Amount: $[Amount]
> Terms: Net 7

## 4. Podcast Episode Invoice (Per-Episode Billing)

> **Podcast Production — Episode [#]**
> Show: [Show Name]
> Description: Recording, editing, and delivery of Episode [#], per agreement dated [Date].
> Amount: $[Amount]
> Terms: Net 7, invoiced on publish

## 5. Podcast Monthly Retainer Invoice (Recurring)

> **Podcast Production — Monthly Retainer**
> Show: [Show Name]
> Package tier: [Basic/Standard/Premium]
> Covers: [X] episodes per month
> Billing period: [Month, Year]
> Amount: $[Monthly rate]
> Terms: Due on the 1st, billed in advance

Set this up as a Subscription/Recurring plan under Invoices → Recurring, not a one-off invoice, so it bills automatically each month.
