**This Agreement** is entered into as of [Date] between **[Your Studio Legal Name]** ("Studio") and **[Client Legal Name]** ("Client").

## 1. Scope of Work

Studio will provide video production services as described in the attached Proposal dated [Date] ("Deliverables"), including pre-production, production (filming), and post-production services.

## 2. Timeline

Studio will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet the milestones in the attached Proposal. Timelines are contingent on Client providing timely feedback, approvals, and any required materials (brand assets, talent availability, location access).

## 3. Fees & Payment Schedule

- Total project fee: **$[Amount]**
- Deposit: **50% ($[Amount])** due upon signing, before work begins
- Balance: **50% ($[Amount])** due upon final delivery, Net 7
- Late payments beyond 14 days may incur a late fee of [X]% per month
- Additional revision rounds, rush delivery, or scope changes beyond what's listed in the Proposal will be invoiced separately as Change Orders

## 4. Revisions

This project includes **[X] rounds of revisions** as specified in the selected package. Additional revision rounds beyond this will be billed at **$[Rate]** per round.

## 5. Cancellation & Rescheduling

- Client may reschedule a confirmed shoot date with at least [X] business days' notice at no charge; less notice may incur a rescheduling fee of **$[Amount]**.
- If Client cancels the project after signing, the deposit is non-refundable, as it covers time and resources already committed.
- If weather, illness, or other conditions outside either party's control require rescheduling a shoot, both parties will agree on a new date in good faith at no additional charge (see Force Majeure, Section 10).

## 6. Usage Rights & Licensing

Upon receipt of full payment, Client is granted usage rights as specified: **[Social Only / Social + Paid Ads / Broadcast / Unlimited-Buyout / Personal Use Only]** (see Proposal/Project record for the specific grant). Studio retains ownership of raw footage and all copyright in the Deliverables except as expressly licensed here. Any use beyond the granted scope (e.g., paid media, broadcast, resale) requires a separate licensing agreement.

## 7. Ownership of Raw Footage

Studio retains ownership of raw, unedited footage. Raw footage is not included in delivery unless purchased as part of the Premium package or as a separate add-on.

## 8. Releases

Client is responsible for obtaining model releases for any non-employee talent appearing on camera and confirming location access/permits, unless Studio has expressly agreed in writing to handle these as part of the Scope of Work.

## 9. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any non-public business information shared during the course of this project, except as required to complete the Deliverables.

## 10. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control, including severe weather, illness, natural disaster, or other circumstances preventing a scheduled shoot or delivery date.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

Studio's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid by Client. Studio is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country].
