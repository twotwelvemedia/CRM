How a project moves through our studio

Every Commercial, Brand Story, and Conference project follows the same seven phases from kickoff to delivery. Here's what happens at each one.

  • 1
    Onboarding & Kickoff
    We confirm your creative brief, your deposit, and get your kickoff call on the calendar.
  • 2
    Pre-Production
    Script, shot list, locations, and crew get locked in ahead of your shoot day.
  • 3
    Production
    Filming and/or photography happens here — this is shoot day (or days).
  • 4
    Post-Production
    Editing, color, sound, and graphics come together behind the scenes.
  • 5
    Review & Revisions
    Your cut is ready — we send it over for feedback, and apply your revisions.
  • 6
    Final Delivery
    Final files are delivered to you in every format your package includes.
  • 7
    Complete
    Project wrapped. We'd love to hear what you thought.
Podcast clients: your show runs on its own episodic cycle instead — topic & scheduling, recording day, editing, your review, then publishing — repeating for every episode. Ask your producer if you'd like the details.
